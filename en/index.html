01 — Services

From design to operations, with a single technical point of contact.

We cover the full cycle of every engineering discipline each project requires: pre-feasibility, design, detailed engineering in BIM, construction supervision, start-up and operations.

E-01

Electrical engineering

Medium- and low-voltage distribution, electrical rooms, switchboards and MCCs, VFDs and soft starters, short-circuit studies, protection coordination, grounding and equipment selection.

I-02

Instrumentation & control

Loop and field instrumentation design, control architecture, PLC and DCS (DeltaV, Siemens TIA Portal, Rockwell PlantPAx/RSLogix) and SCADA (Wonderware).

D-03

Detailed engineering & BIM modeling

Engineering from conceptual to detailed design, with 3D BIM modeling of electrical and instrumentation systems in Revit: conduit routing, installation typicals and control architectures. Schematics in EPLAN and lighting design in DIALux.

Q-04

Process engineering

Analysis and optimization of lithium processes: brines, evaporation, lithium carbonate and DLE technologies. Bottleneck resolution and operating standards.

C-05

Commissioning & start-up

Pre-commissioning, loop checks, functional testing, energization and plant start-up. As-built documentation and handover to operations.

O-06

Operations & continuous improvement

Plant stabilization, production, cost and safety KPI tracking, Lean and Six Sigma. Operating manuals, procedures and team training.

P-07

Project management & supervision

Technical management of contractors, scheduling in MS Project and Primavera, scope, schedule and quality control. Owner’s engineer services.

A-08

Support for international companies

Local technical partner for foreign vendors and EPCs entering Argentina and Paraguay: regulations, suppliers, logistics and site management. We work in English and Spanish.

06 — Aquatech representation

Aquatech technology for water, energy and critical minerals.

We represent Aquatech in Argentina and Paraguay. Founded in 1981 in the United States, Aquatech is a global leader in water purification technology for industry, with more than 1,000 projects in over 60 countries.

  • DesalinationSeawater and brackish water reverse osmosis, and thermal desalination for industry and utilities.
  • Water & wastewater reuseMembrane systems, such as HERO™ high-efficiency reverse osmosis, to recover water from industrial effluents.
  • Zero liquid discharge (ZLD)Membranes, evaporators and crystallizers combined so that no effluent is discharged. More than 160 installations on 6 continents.
  • Oil & gas produced waterTreatment and reuse of oilfield produced water, including unconventional plays.
  • Process & ultrapure waterDemineralized water for power generation, chemicals and petrochemicals, and ultrapure water for semiconductors.
  • Lithium & critical mineralsLithium extraction, concentration and refining with the PEARL platform.
