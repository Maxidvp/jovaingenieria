01 — Services

From design to operations, with a single technical point of contact.

We cover the full electrical, control and process engineering cycle: design, construction supervision, start-up and operations.

E-01

Electrical engineering

Medium- and low-voltage distribution, switchboards and MCCs, short-circuit studies, protection coordination, grounding and equipment selection.

I-02

Instrumentation & control

Loop and field instrumentation design, control architecture, PLC and DCS (DeltaV, Siemens TIA Portal, Rockwell PlantPAx/RSLogix) and SCADA (Wonderware).

E-03

VFDs & soft starters

Specification, parameterization and commissioning of VFDs and soft starters for pumps, compressors and conveyors. Troubleshooting and root-cause analysis.

Q-04

Process engineering

Analysis and optimization of lithium processes: brines, evaporation, lithium carbonate and DLE technologies. Bottleneck resolution and operating standards.

C-05

Commissioning & start-up

Pre-commissioning, loop checks, functional testing, energization and plant start-up. As-built documentation and handover to operations.

O-06

Operations & continuous improvement

Plant stabilization, production, cost and safety KPI tracking, Lean and Six Sigma. Operating manuals, procedures and team training.

P-07

Project management & supervision

Technical management of contractors, scheduling in MS Project and Primavera, scope, schedule and quality control. Owner’s engineer services.

A-08

Support for international companies

Local technical partner for foreign vendors and EPCs entering Argentina and Paraguay: regulations, suppliers, logistics and site management. We work in English and Spanish.
